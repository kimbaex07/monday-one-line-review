## The entry

> Monday: my alarm filed for PTO, my coffee tasted like a deadline, and my inbox promoted itself to my manager.

**Character count: 109** (limit: under 200).

## Why it lands

Three concrete Monday miseries, each escalated one notch past normal: the alarm doesn't just ring, it files for PTO — your own wake-up call has quit on you; the coffee doesn't just taste bad, it tastes like a deadline; the inbox doesn't just pile up, it gets promoted above you. The joke builds from object to object, and the last one lands the punch: by the end of the line, even your email outranks you. It reads as a review of one real Monday rather than a generic complaint, and it's short enough to repeat to a coworker the next morning.

## How to run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```

`main.py` is pure Python stdlib, deterministic, and writes the single line (plus a trailing newline) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
